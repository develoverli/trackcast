Welcome to TrackCast

Display your currently playing Spotify track as an overlay in OBS Studio.

  • Works with Spotify and OBS WebSocket

    Seamless integration for your streaming setup.

  • Automatically updates as songs change

Step 1 of 4

Connect Spotify

TrackCast uses your own free Spotify Developer app to read what you're playing. It takes about two minutes.

  1. 1

    Create an app in the Spotify Developer Dashboard

    Sign in with your Spotify account, then click Create app.

  2. 2

    Fill in the form with these values

    App name
    App description
    Website optional
    Redirect URIs

    Paste the redirect URI and click Add. Under Which API/SDKs are you planning to use? check Web API, accept the terms, then click Save.

  3. 3

    Paste your app credentials

    In your new app, open Settings and copy the Client ID and Client secret.

  4. 4

    Authorize TrackCast

    Your browser opens Spotify. Approve access, then come back here.

    Spotify connected

Step 2 of 4

Connect OBS

TrackCast talks to OBS Studio over WebSocket to add and update your overlay.

  1. 1

    Turn on the OBS WebSocket server

    In OBS Studio open Tools → WebSocket Server Settings, check Enable WebSocket server, then click Show Connect Info to see the port and password.

    Enter the connection details
